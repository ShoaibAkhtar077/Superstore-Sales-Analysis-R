# Superstore Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the Superstore sales dataset using R to identify patterns in sales, profitability, regional performance, product categories, and monthly sales trends.

The analysis was conducted using R and R Markdown, with data manipulation and visualization performed using the tidyverse ecosystem.

## Objectives

- Analyze total sales by product category
- Analyze profit by product category
- Compare sales and profit across regions
- Identify the most and least profitable sub-categories
- Examine monthly sales trends
- Analyze the distribution of sales
- Generate business-oriented insights from the data

## Tools & Technologies

- R
- RStudio
- R Markdown
- tidyverse
- ggplot2
- dplyr
- lubridate
- scales
- knitr

## Analysis

### Sales by Category

The analysis compares total sales across Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology.

### Regional Performance

Sales and profit are compared across the four regions to identify differences in regional performance.

### Sub-Category Profitability

Sub-category-level analysis is used to identify products that contribute strongly to profit and those with weaker profitability.

### Monthly Sales Trend

Monthly aggregation is used to examine changes in sales over time.

## Key Insights

- Technology generates the highest sales among the three major categories.
- Office Supplies also contributes strongly to total sales.
- Furniture generates relatively high sales but has a much lower profit margin.
- Regional analysis highlights differences between sales and profitability.
- Monthly analysis provides insight into changes in sales over time.
